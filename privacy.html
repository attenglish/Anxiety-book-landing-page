3. About the interactive tool

The tool asks ten short questions about how you feel when speaking English. Your answers are used only in your browser, to show you your results on screen. They are not sent to us, not saved, and not linked to your email address. When you close or leave the page, they are gone.

The tool is a starting point for reflection. It is not a medical assessment or a diagnosis.

5. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your information. We use trusted companies to run our services. They process your information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service:

  • MailerLite, which stores your name and email address and sends our emails.
  • Netlify, which hosts this website.
  • TidyCal and Google Calendar, which handle class bookings and scheduling.
  • Stripe and PayPal, which process payments for classes.
  • Patreon, if you choose to join our membership.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the fonts on this page. When the page loads, your browser contacts Google, which means your IP address is shared with Google.

We may also share information where the law requires it, for example with tax authorities or if ordered by a court.

Some of these companies are based outside the UK, including in the United States. Where your information is transferred abroad, we rely on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses.

6. Cookies and tracking

This page does not set its own cookies and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. If you follow a link to a third-party service such as TidyCal, Stripe, PayPal or Patreon, that service may use cookies under its own policy.

7. How long we keep your information

  • Subscriber details (name and email): until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. After you unsubscribe we may keep your email address on a suppression list so that we do not email you again.
  • Booking and payment records: for as long as needed for tax and accounting, normally six years.
  • Emails you send us: for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, and then for a reasonable period afterwards.

  • ask for your information in a portable format, where it applies;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting anything we did before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, email [your contact email]. We will reply within one month.

9. Children

Our tool and services are aimed at adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you think a child has given us their details, please contact us and we will delete them.

10. Keeping your information safe

We use reputable providers and sensible security measures to protect your information. No online service can be completely secure, but we take care to limit who can see your data and to keep it only as long as we need it.

11. Complaints

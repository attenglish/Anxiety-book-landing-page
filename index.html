Three things to try this week

  1. Prepare your first sentence only, not the whole conversation. A steady start does most of the work.
  2. Slow down by ten per cent. A slightly slower pace calms your breathing and makes you clearer.
  3. Keep a recovery phrase ready: "Let me say that again." Having it makes mistakes far less frightening.

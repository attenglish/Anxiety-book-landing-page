Most learners fixate on doing more, while ignoring the two conditions that actually make practice work: comprehensible input and low anxiety. Below, try reframing a few outcome goals — the vague, perfectionistic kind — into the process goals this chapter recommends.

A familiar scene

James's story

Pushed into a meetup, then found a coffee chat

James forced himself into a conversation meetup where everyone seemed more confident than him, watched films without subtitles, and obsessed over every message he sent. After two weeks he was exhausted and stopped practising altogether.

Months later a colleague invited him to an informal weekly coffee chat with two other learners — low pressure, lots of laughing when someone couldn't find a word. After four weeks, James was speaking more freely than he had in years. Nothing about his English had changed. What changed was the environment he practised in.

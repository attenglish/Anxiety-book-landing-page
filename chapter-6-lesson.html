Part 1

What repeated exposure looks like

Each ladder step should create manageable anxiety — roughly levels 4 to 7, not complete comfort and not complete overwhelm. You repeat a step until it feels significantly easier, then move up. Prepare across three dimensions before each attempt: practical (remove uncertainty wherever possible), physical (use the calming techniques from Chapter 1), and psychological ("this is supposed to feel uncomfortable — that means I'm learning").

Part 1

Spot the low-stakes opportunity

Low-stakes interactions share a few features: they're brief, repeatable, and don't cost much if they go imperfectly. Recognising them is most of the work — the rest is just choosing them on purpose instead of avoiding them. Have a go at sorting these.

A familiar scene

The broken self-checkout

Two years in London, avoiding every unscripted conversation

She'd quietly built a system: self-checkout only, ordering apps, nodding at the gym rather than risking conversation. Then every self-checkout was broken one Saturday. She queued. "Hello, how are you today?" Her prepared answer vanished. "I'm... good, thank you." She caught perhaps 60% of what came next, but managed a reply. The cashier laughed kindly.

By the fourth Saturday of deliberately choosing the human till, the dread had mostly gone. What was left was ordinary, manageable nervousness — the kind that doesn't stop you doing something.

A familiar scene

Marcus's story

Interview one: memorised. Interview two: prepared.

Marcus wrote detailed answers to every possible question and rehearsed until they were word-perfect. In the real interview, a follow-up question interrupted his script — his mind blanked. He didn't get the job.

Three months later, for a second opportunity, he prepared key themes rather than exact answers, and his nervous system alongside his content. When he stumbled, he repaired naturally and continued. He got the job. Flexible readiness beats memorisation that breaks under pressure.

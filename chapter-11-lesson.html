A familiar scene

Phone settings and gardening forums

Three weeks after an intensive course ended

She'd barely touched English despite good intentions. What actually worked was almost accidental: switching her phone to English, listening to podcasts about subjects she already loved on her commute, joining an English-speaking gardening forum because gardening was her hobby, not because it was good practice.

Three months on, she'd used English nearly every day, but it hadn't felt like studying. It had felt like living her normal life, just with the language switched.

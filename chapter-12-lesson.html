    A familiar scene

    Ling's story

    Reviewing six months of progress

    When Ling tries to assess her progress, her mind immediately focuses on what she still can't do. Her partner reminds her: "Six months ago, you wouldn't speak in meetings at all. Last week, you led one. You used to spend hours agonising over every email. Yesterday, you sent three work emails in twenty minutes."

    She pulls out her original notebook. Almost every situation once rated 8, 9, or 10 on anxiety is now manageable. Growth isn't measured by arrival at perfection — it's measured by the distance between where you started and where you are now.

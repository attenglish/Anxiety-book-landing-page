Chen's story

Six months of progress, then a restructuring

Speaking in meetings felt impossible six months ago; now Chen contributes regularly. Then a major restructuring brings a new director, and the first meeting is entirely in English. Chen freezes. "I'm back at the beginning. All that progress for nothing."

His partner offers a different read: "Maybe this is just a difficult period. The whole company is stressed right now." Chen returns to the techniques that helped before. Over three weeks, his comfort returns — and he learns something important: he knows how to recover now.
